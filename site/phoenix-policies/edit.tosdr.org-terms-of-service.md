## Rules

We have a set of rules for Our Community that should be common sense:

- **No illegal content.** This includes material involving minors.
- **No spamming, junk content or promotional messages.** We have a zero-tolerance policy against spammers, as maintaining this Site is made more difficult from it.
- **No harassment, no threats.** This includes posting any material that constitutes hate speech, discrimination, xenophobia, threats of violence, or similar content.
- **No doxxing, or anything that infringes upon the privacy of others.** We value privacy, so Users are expected to follow this standard aswell.
- **No hacking.** We do not tolerate hacking, both targeted against Us or Our users.

If a User breaks any of these rules We will act accordingly, with a proportionate mindset.
Breaking Our rules will result in the removal of the offending content and/or the suspension of the offending User.
If Your account is suspended You are not allowed to create an alternative account to circumvent said ban.

so please be patient while Staff works on that.

The ToS;DR Staff reserves the right to terminate Your account if You do not adhere to these terms or [Our Community Etiquette](https://docs.tosdr.org/site-policy/community-etiquette).
We will issue terminations fairly and with due reasoning. If Your account has been wrongfully terminated
please send Us an email for an appeal. We reserve the right to reject Your appeal if Your termination warrants a permanent ban
(e.g. engaging in spam, hacking, and other severe violations).
