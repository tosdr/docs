# edit.tosdr.org Privacy Policy

**LAST UPDATED: 2026-06-12**

---

## What Information We Collect and How It Is Collected

When creating an account You are only required to provide an email address, a password and a unique username.
We reserve the right to delete Your account if its username:

- Is offensive, demeaning, threatening or hateful
- Constitutes as spam or advertising
- Contains Personal Information of an unconsenting third party
- Is illegal, harmful towards minors
- Contains sexually explicit, pornographic, lewd or obscene content
- Is reserved or resembles one that is reserved, such as _"admin"_, _"team"_, _"staff"_ or any Staff member's username or identity

You are not required to provide Your real identity or any other Personal Information. If provided, You understand that
You are providing it at Your own risk. Please be careful when doing so, as this site is public and can be viewed
by anyone.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We only use the information provided by You to manage Your account, monitor any suspicious activity,
and to associate it with Your User Contributions.

We have not, do not, and never will use Your Personal Information to:

- **Sell, rent or distribute it to third parties.**
- **Use third-party trackers/cookies.**
- **Share it with third-party analytics services.**
- **Display advertisements on any of Our platforms, first-party or otherwise.**
- **Track You or Your identity.**

---

## Security

You are responsible for properly securing Your account. We will try Our best to notify You in case
We find out that Your account has been breached. Nevertheless, You must notify Us in case of unauthorized access
to Your account.

Passwords are hashed in Our database with the `bcrypt` algorithm.

---

## Account Deletion

You are free to close Your account through Your Account Settings at any time. When closing Your account
We will scramble Your data in Our database, so You will not be able to recover Your account.
Your contributions will remain featured on the Site, unless You ask Us to remove them.
They will not be tied to Your identity once Your account is closed, and will not display any of Your
Personal Information unless You specifically add it to a contribution.

If You wish to have Your content deleted, please send Us a request at [team@tosdr.org](mailto:team@tosdr.org).

---

## Cookies

We only use first-party cookies. A cookie is a small file containing a string of characters that is sent to Your computer when You visit a website.

When visiting this Site, Our cookies allow Us to recognize Your browser, to prevent spam, DDoS attacks and other malicious activity.
If You tick the "Remember Me" checkbox, they will also store Your login information. They may also store user preferences.

You can configure Your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent.
Bear in mind that disabling cookies might affect Your login attempts, but all other features of the site shall remain unaffected.
